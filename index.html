Questions sellers ask

Don’t see yours? Email us.

Who is Within Group?

Within Group LLC is a New York–based real estate credit firm. We source and coordinate acquisitions of non-performing commercial real estate and multifamily loans on behalf of a dedicated institutional capital partner, working with regional and community banks, debt funds and mortgage REITs, CMBS and CLO special servicers, life companies, and their loan sale advisors.

What loans fit the buy box?

First-lien whole loans with $3MM+ of UPB per asset (target $5–7MM, no cap), secured by multifamily, mixed-use, office, industrial, self-storage, or 2–4 unit residential property with in-place cash flow, at or below 80% LTV on current as-is value. Retail and hospitality are considered selectively.

Which loan types and default statuses do you consider?

Bridge and transitional, CRE term, mini-perm, CMBS conduit, life company, and DSCR loans, whether held on balance sheet or securitized. The loan should be in monetary default, maturity default, or foreclosure.

Do you buy REO?

No. Our capital partner acquires notes only. Also outside the box: single-family (1-unit) residential, owner-occupied and owner-user properties (including SBA loans), rent-regulated multifamily, in-process construction or renovation, and land or entitlement plays.

Do you take part in marketed loan sales?

Yes. We trade bilaterally off-market under NDA, and we also bid in marketed, advisor-run sale processes. Add us to the bidder list for your next sale.

Do you bid on single loans or pools?

Both. Bids are made at the loan level, whether the notes come one at a time or as a pool, so each loan is priced on its own collateral.

Which states do you buy in?

Nationwide, with an emphasis on lender-friendly jurisdictions.

How are bids priced, and how quickly can a trade close?

Each loan is priced on its current as-is value from an independent valuation. Bids are all-cash with no financing contingency. Once bid and ask align and an LOI is signed, diligence is a 5–7 day data room review, followed by a loan sale agreement and closing.

What should a loan tape include?

Property address and type, UPB, note rate and default rate, maturity date, last paid date, accrued interest, occupancy or rent roll, the most recent valuation, and legal or foreclosure status. A single-asset summary is a fine place to start.

Is Within Group a broker-dealer?

No. Within Group sources and coordinates loan sale transactions on behalf of its capital partner and does not offer or solicit securities. All indications of interest are non-binding and subject to diligence, documentation, and capital partner approval.
